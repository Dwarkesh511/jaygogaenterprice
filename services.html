Service

CNC Machining Services

Multi-axis milling for prismatic components where bore relationships, surface finish, and positional tolerances must hold across lots. Ideal for prototypes, first articles, and sustained production releases.

  • Tooling strategies and stable setups for predictable cycle times
  • In-process dimensional checks aligned to your inspection plan
  • Deburr, clean, and protect for line-ready delivery

Service

VMC Machining

Vertical machining centers provide rigidity for heavy cuts and accuracy for finishing passes—well suited to housings, manifolds, brackets, and fixturing with complex feature sets.

  • Workholding approaches that protect datum relationships
  • Efficient roughing/finishing separation for demanding geometries
  • Measured releases with documentation for critical characteristics

Service

Laser Cutting Services

Precision two-dimensional profiles in carbon steel, stainless, and aluminum—with nesting discipline to support fabrication throughput and material efficiency.

  • Clean edge quality for downstream forming and welding
  • Kit labeling and staging options for complex BOMs
  • Integration with fabrication for welded assemblies

Service

Precision Engineering & Components

Precision engineering support for critical interfaces—shafts, housings, mounting features, and hardware—where documentation, traceability, and measurement discipline reduce risk on your line.

  • Drawing interpretation and practical tolerance planning
  • First-article and in-process inspection strategies
  • Protective packaging options for precision surfaces

Custom Precision Manufacturing

Custom manufacturing for low- to mid-volume industrial programs—combining machining, laser, and fabrication disciplines under controlled routings to reduce handling and protect schedules.

  • DFM feedback before metal is cut
  • Prototype builds with measurable learning for scale-up
  • Lot control and traceability when your package requires it

Industrial Fabrication Solutions

Industrial fabrication for frames, bases, guarding, and weldments—planned for squareness, weld access, and downstream machining interfaces when your assembly demands it.

  • Hardware installation and travel-ready packaging
